Coach's Notes

    Scoring reads your movement's speed, smoothness and stability from the webcam feed via on-device pose tracking — it's a motion coach, not a certified Michael Jackson judge. Nothing is recorded or uploaded; all tracking runs locally in your browser.

    How To Play

    1. Tap Step On Stage and allow camera access when your browser asks.
    2. Pick a move from the dropdown — each one is scored on different things, so check the guide below before you go for a high score.
    3. Tap Record 5s Take. You'll get a 3-2-1 countdown, then 5 seconds to perform.
    4. Stay fully in frame — the gold skeleton overlay shows what the camera can see. If a limb disappears, step back.
    5. Your score, a metric breakdown, and coaching tips appear right after your take.
    6. Tap Record 5s Take again to retry the same move, or pick a new one. Your recent scores are tracked at the bottom of the Stage tab.

    Move Guide

    Moonwalk Medium

    Glide backward while your feet look like they're sliding forward. Keep your torso still and upright, push off the ball of your back foot, and keep knees soft. Scored on glide, smoothness, control and energy.

    Watch on YouTube
    Reference: Michael Jackson performing the Moonwalk.

    The Spin Medium

    A fast whip turn that finishes balanced. Spot a fixed point with your eyes, snap your head around first, and land on the balls of your feet with soft knees. Scored on energy, smoothness, balance and finish.

    Watch on YouTube
    Reference: Michael Jackson performing The Spin.

    Anti-Gravity Lean Hard

    Lean forward past what looks physically possible, then hold it. Keep a straight line from ankles to head — the lean comes from the ankles, not the waist. Scored on hold, control, commitment and smoothness. Don't actually try the full 45° angle without the special shoes — Jackson used a patented harness rig for the real thing.

    Watch on YouTube
    Reference: Michael Jackson's Anti-Gravity Lean.

    Point & Freeze Easy

    Snap into a pointing pose, fully extended, and hold dead still. Commit fast, then don't move a muscle. Scored on snap speed, freeze stillness, extension and control.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: MJ's signature freeze poses across performances.

    Toe Stand Hard

    Rise slowly onto your toes and hold your balance at the top. Keep ankles stacked under your hips and brace your core. Scored on balance, hold, control and energy.

    Watch on YouTube
    Reference: Michael Jackson's Toe Stand.

    The Kick Easy

    A sharp, high kick with a clean snap-back to standing. Drive the kick up fast, keep your standing leg stable, and stick the landing rather than wobbling. Scored on snap, height, landing control and balance.

    Watch on YouTube
    Reference: Michael Jackson's Kick.

    The Robot Medium

    Mechanical, segmented movement — each joint moves and stops on its own, no fluid blending between them. Isolate one joint at a time and hit hard stops. Scored on isolation, sharpness, hold-per-beat and control.

    Watch on YouTube
    Reference: Michael Jackson's Robot.

    Head Snap Easy

    Whip your head sharply to one side or angle on the beat, then stop it dead — no drift. Keep your shoulders still so it reads as an isolated head movement, not a whole-body flinch. Scored on snap sharpness, precision of the stop, isolation and timing.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: sharp head isolations across MJ's performances.

    Side Glide Medium

    Step sideways smoothly, feet low to the ground with no vertical bounce, crossing one foot behind or in front of the other in a continuous line. Keep your upper body level and relaxed. Scored on glide, smoothness, consistency and energy.

    Watch on YouTube
    Reference: Michael Jackson's Side Glide.

    Crotch Grab Easy

    A sharp, punctuated accent move — snap into it on the beat, hold it briefly, then release. Keep the rest of your body still around the accent so it reads as a clean, confident hit rather than a wiggle. Scored on snap, hold, control and confidence.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: one of MJ's most recognizable signature accents.

    Group Unison Hit Medium

    A single big, sharp movement landed on one beat, then a hard freeze right after — the kind of hit a whole group of dancers lands together in sync. Commit your entire body to one clean, powerful motion rather than several small ones. Scored on hit power, freeze stillness, clarity and timing.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: the synchronized group choreography hit from "Beat It."

    Hat Tip Easy

    A smooth reach up followed by a sharp, confident flick of the brim. Keep your posture upright and add a brief held beat right after the tip so the accent lands cleanly rather than trailing off. Scored on reach, flick sharpness, hold and style.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: one of MJ's classic hat-tip flourishes.

    Jacket Move Easy

    A sharp, shoulder-driven pop or tug of the jacket — a quick, punchy accent rather than a slow adjustment. Keep the rest of your body still so the motion reads clearly, and finish in a confident held pose. Scored on pop sharpness, punch, finish and control.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: MJ's signature jacket-accent styling.

    Knee Drop Medium

    A controlled drop down onto one or both knees, then a hold before rising back up. Keep your back straight and core braced through the descent rather than collapsing. Scored on control, descent, hold and recovery.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: a signature MJ knee drop moment.

    Foot Stomp Easy

    A sharp, decisive stomp of one foot down on the beat. Keep your upper body steady so the accent reads clearly, and stay balanced on the standing leg. Scored on impact, balance, sharpness and recovery.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: MJ's percussive foot-stomp accents.

    Circle Glide Medium

    A smooth, continuous glide tracing a circular path with your feet, low to the ground with no straight-line stepping. Keep the pace even throughout. Scored on glide, smoothness, consistency and energy.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: MJ's circular gliding footwork.

    Jackson Shuffle Hard

    Quick, light, compact footwork — small fast steps rather than big movements, staying loose and bouncy on your feet. Scored on footwork speed, rhythm, lightness and control.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: MJ's rapid, light footwork sequences.

    Fedora Tilt Easy

    A slower, more deliberate tilt of the hat brim than the quick Hat Tip — reach up, tilt with control, and hold the pose for a beat before releasing. Scored on reach, tilt control, hold and style.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: MJ's classic fedora styling.

    Side Walk Easy

    Deliberate sideways steps with a clear walking rhythm — steadier and more grounded than the Side Glide. Keep each step even and on-beat, with a relaxed upper body. Scored on rhythm, step clarity, balance and style.

    Search YouTube
    Reference: MJ's grounded side-step walking rhythm.
